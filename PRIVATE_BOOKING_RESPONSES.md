# Private appointment replies

The external-AI boundary is in `apps/api/src/ai-context.ts`. All DeepSeek conversational, approved-answer selection, fallback classification and booking-extraction builders use approved context. Messages have explicit provenance: user, normal assistant, calendar, or unclassified. Calendar responses and legacy generated/staff messages fail closed; only independent customer/normal assistant history and customer-sourced preferred-name memory can leave the backend. Unknown summaries are not approved context. Current forwarded copies of known protected replies and confirmation codes are also redacted.

Migration 013 adds one provenance column and backfills customer ingress only. It does not delete history, alter credentials or fabricate legacy assistant provenance. The inbox and delivery paths stamp new messages; booking state stays in the existing persisted history and appointment request records. No new booking service or dependency is introduced. Apply this additive migration before starting the new API. Historical generated responses remain visible to staff but are intentionally excluded from external AI context because their origin cannot be proven safe.

The appointment extractor receives customer booking text, not connected timezone/calendar settings/results. It extracts relative days, a weekday, literal civil date/time and ambiguity. The backend resolves those values using its booking settings and server clock, validates the configured hours, and alone calls Google. AM/PM is inferred only when exactly one interpretation fits the configured future booking hours; otherwise the customer is asked to clarify. Routine status replies make no external AI request.

`booking-replies.ts` supplies short English, Roman Urdu and Urdu variations for available/proposed, unavailable, checked alternative, confirmation required, confirmed, failed, uncertain, missing date/time, clinic closed, cancellation and staff-assistance states. Variation selection is deterministic from the event/state, so retrying does not randomly change the response. Proposed slots explicitly remain unreserved and include CONFIRM/CANCEL instructions. Alternative searches run only when requested and check at most three in-hours candidates. A successful booking response requires verified event creation; creating/uncertain states never claim success.

Existing confirmation codes, tenant checks, free/busy recheck, deterministic Google event IDs, write reconciliation, staff handoff and transport inbox deduplication remain. A replayed proposal is loaded from its persisted source-event row; it does not cancel itself or silently turn a previous failure into a fresh offer. Backend-only booking replies can appear in staff history without reentering AI prompts.

Independent user-entered messages/business rules are still AI inputs. Do not paste private Calendar exports into those inputs. The boundary protects application-originated Calendar data flows; it cannot establish the provenance of arbitrary information a person manually types. Google consent is retained and public disclosures updated; no Google approval is claimed. VPS ports, Caddy, OAuth credentials and encryption keys are unchanged.
